# Chitrakote: a Living Screencast

45 s · 1080×1920 vertical · 30 fps · English narration · burned-in captions + `.srt`

**The app.** The style needs a real interface, and no real product is the subject here, so the film shows an **original, fictional trip-planner app, "Pagdandi"** (Hindi for "footpath"). It is drawn as a plain mobile-style planner: search, place card, route, facts, season switch, save. Every number on screen is sourced (`FACTS.md`).

**The mascot.** Pip, an original pixel explorer (a 12×14 grid: teal hat, orange backpack, two-pixel eyes). Teal and orange are never used by the app UI (which is slate and indigo), so Pip finds the eye in every frame. Pip is the app's agency: it reads, routes, measures, and saves.

## 1. Three structures

1. **A single journey through the planner.** Search → place → route → facts → season → save. Clear, but flat: every chapter weighs the same.
2. **Countdown checklist.** A packing list ticks down to departure. Fun, but the falls only arrive at the very end, and the "comes alive" moment would be a postscript.
3. **One switch that changes everything.** The planner builds the trip calmly in *Summer* mode: a thin, quiet falls in a small photo card. The turn is the season switch to *Monsoon*: the Indravati swells, the horseshoe fills edge to edge, and the water breaks out of the card and floods the whole screen ("the film changes state"). Then it pours back into the card, and the trip is saved for July.

**Chosen: 3**, with 1 as its first half. The turn is a fact (the falls reach nearly 300 m wide only in the monsoon), so the spectacle is also the useful information: *when to go*.

- **Opening:** empty state. A blank search field, caret blinking; Pip drops from the status bar onto it. (Not a logo reveal.)
- **Ending:** a real save. The "Trip saved · Jul–Oct" toast slides up; Pip rides it, then sits on the saved card and the falls keep roaring in the thumbnail.
- **Score shape:** sparse electric-piano + chiptune duet → drums drop out before the switch (silence) → full band and a big low "flood" swell at the switch → settles to a warm outro.

## 2. Logline and arc

A small pixel explorer plans a trip to Chitrakote and discovers that the date matters more than the destination.

- **Setup (0–17 s):** search, place, route from Jagdalpur, the facts. The falls are a thin summer trickle in the card.
- **Turn (17–31 s):** "But when?" Silence. The cursor flips Summer → Monsoon. The falls widen into a 300 m horseshoe and flood the screen.
- **Ending (31–45 s):** the water drains back into the card; "Niagara of India" spotlight; trip saved for July–October; Pip sits down to watch.

## 3. Benchmark

- **Learn from** Screen Studio-style app recordings (auto-zoom to the caret, smooth cursor, push-in on clicks, window floating on wallpaper) and Apple's "Guided Tour" pacing (one feature per sentence, calm voice).
- **Learn from** Duolingo-style mascot acting: anticipation crouch, squash on landing, eyes looking at what matters.
- **Don't take:** any app's layout, icons, characters or branding; no real travel-app look-alike. The planner is our own.

## 6. Cue map

- 100 BPM, 4/4: one beat = 0.6 s, one bar = 2.4 s. Bars start at 0.0.
- **A (0–19.2, bars 1–8):** electric piano chords (I–vi–IV–V in D major), upright-style synth bass, brushed kit from bar 3. Chip square doubles Pip's jumps (one note per landing).
- **B (19.2–24.0, bars 9–10):** drums out, a single held pad; 22.6–24.0 near-silence, room tone.
- **C (24.0–33.6, bars 11–14):** the switch click on the downbeat of bar 11 (24.0); full band, low swell + roar bed; chip lead plays the melody in duet with piano.
- **D (33.6–45.0, bars 15–19):** softer, piano + chip trade phrases, final chord at 43.2, ring out to 45.0.
- Cuts/chapter wipes on downbeats: 9.6, 14.4, 19.2, 24.0, 36.0 (pill wipes ride these).

## 8. Type and captions

- UI type: **Inter** (OFL), slate on near-white, indigo accent (#4F46E5) for buttons and the switch. Pip is teal/orange; nothing in the UI uses those.
- Captions: frosted-glass pill near the bottom, 44 px Inter SemiBold, never over the element being discussed (the camera keeps the subject in the top two-thirds). Hold ≥ max(1.8 s, speech + 0.6 s).
- Chapter pill top-left of the phone (01 Search · 02 Route · 03 Facts · 04 Save), key HUD bottom-centre above captions while typing.
- The title is diegetic: the planner's empty state reads "Where to next?".
