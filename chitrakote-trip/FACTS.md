# Facts used in the film

| Claim in the film | Source | Wording |
|---|---|---|
| Chitrakote is on the Indravati River, Bastar district, Chhattisgarh | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chitrakote_Falls | "a natural waterfall on the Indravati River … in the Bastar district of Chhattisgarh" |
| 38 km from Jagdalpur | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chitrakote_Falls ; https://www.holidify.com/places/jagdalpur/chitrakote-falls-sightseeing-1254523.html | "approximately 38 km to the west of Jagdalpur" (some sources say 40 km) |
| About 29 m high | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chitrakote_Falls | "height of the falls is about 29 m (95 ft)" (other sources give 30–32 m) |
| Widest waterfall in India; nearly 300 m wide in the monsoon | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chitrakote_Falls ; https://www.nativeplanet.com/travel-guide/all-about-indias-widest-waterfall-the-chitrakoot-falls-003907.html | "the widest fall in India … during the monsoon, the width of the falls reaches nearly 300 m (980 ft)" |
| Called the "Niagara of India"; horseshoe shape | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chitrakote_Falls ; https://www.outlooktraveller.com/amp/story/celebrating-people/experiences/nature/guide-to-chitrakote-waterfalls-in-chhattisgarh-niagara-falls-of-india | "often referred to as the Niagara Falls of India"; "horseshoe-shaped" |
| Best time July–October (monsoon) | https://www.icecubes.in/places/chitrakote-waterfalls-the-niagara-falls-of-india-in-chhattisgarh ; https://www.holidify.com/places/jagdalpur/chitrakote-falls-sightseeing-1254523.html | monsoon, July to October, flow at its peak; summer flow reduced |

Monsoon water of the Indravati is muddy brown; the film colours the monsoon falls tea-brown with white foam, and the summer falls thin and white.
